**Description** (4000 max):

TimeFlow shows your day as a river. A NOW line stays in place while your tasks drift toward it and flow past as the minutes go by. You always see what's happening now, what's next, and what's already behind you, without the stress of a cramped grid.

FLOWS WITH YOU
• The timeline keeps the present in view on its own
• Scroll ahead or back any time, then tap once to return to now
• Days run together, with sunrise and sunset marked
• Pinch to zoom from the big picture to five-minute detail

PLAN IN SECONDS
• Long-press anywhere on the timeline to add a task at that time
• Long-press a task and drag it to move it
• Swipe right when it's done, left to delete (with undo)
• Notes, a photo, your own categories and an "important" flag when you need them

REPEATS THAT MAKE SENSE
• Daily, weekdays, chosen days of the week, every few weeks, monthly, yearly
• Change just one occurrence, that one and the rest, or the whole series
• Repeating tasks keep their time when the clocks change

GENTLE REMINDERS
• Notifications before tasks start, even when the app is closed
• Mark done or snooze right from the notification

HAND OVER YOUR DAY
Going away? Share a day or a week with a pet sitter, babysitter, caregiver or family member as a link. They open it in any browser and see the same live river, with nothing to install and no account to make. The schedule travels inside the link; it's never uploaded anywhere.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything stays on your iPhone or iPad
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Back up and restore with a file whenever you like

Made for anyone who finds a calendar grid stressful, and especially helpful for visual thinkers and people with ADHD who want to see time pass.

**What's New in This Version** (1.0.0):
First release on iPhone and iPad: the flowing timeline, repeating tasks, reminders, photos, link sharing and backups.
