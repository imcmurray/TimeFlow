TimeFlow support

Help for TimeFlow on iPhone, iPad, Android, desktop and the web.

TimeFlow is made by Rinse Repeat Labs, Ian McMurray's studio, and is published on the App Store under Ian McMurray.

Get in touch

Found a bug, or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your device, its system version and the TimeFlow version (Settings → About).

Reminders don't arrive

  • TimeFlow asks for notification permission the first time you set a reminder. If you said no, turn notifications on for TimeFlow in your device's Settings.
  • Check that the task has a reminder set (open the task → Reminder).
  • Focus modes and Do Not Disturb can hold notifications back.

Moving to a new phone

Your tasks live only on your device. In TimeFlow, go to Settings → Back up tasks and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new device (Files, iCloud Drive, email to yourself). On the new device, use Settings → Restore from backup.

Sharing your day

Tap the share button on the timeline to send a day or a week as a link. The person you send it to opens it in any browser, with nothing to install. The schedule travels inside the link and isn't uploaded anywhere.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all tasks removes every task. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Privacy

TimeFlow has no accounts, ads or tracking, and your tasks never leave your device. Read the privacy policy.
